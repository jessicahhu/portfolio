San Francisco Restaurant Power Ranking

November 2026

showcasing all of my favorite places in SF - recent discoveries and childhood favorites, in no particular order.

most recently updated: Nov. 2026

casual

  • lou's cafe on taraval

    SF sandwich culture is elite & there are so many amazing delis in the city. I've been coming here since I was 10, getting pretty much the exact same thing: Roma's Club with extra red onions and jalapeño spread.

  • naya dessert cafe

    Anyone who knows me knows my odd distaste for sweets. Naya is an exception. I'm obsessed with the creamy shaved ice flavor and texture + the graham cracker crumbles on the strawberry bingsu. A+!!

  • khao tiew

    Now, I would never wait 3 hours for this. The hack is to order take out: crispy papaya salad, khao pad mun nuer, and kaya toast.

  • arsicault on arguello

    I'm a pastry fiend. I could eat croissants for every meal and every day if there was enough nutritional value. The plain croissant is my second favorite pastry in the world.

  • the laundromat

    Also a bagel fiend, a true lover of baked goods. I've tried most (hyped up) bagels in NYC, but not much compares.

  • kevin & chris's noodle house (all locations)

    Fastest service ever??? If you're in a jiffy and looking for a warm, comforting, and cheap meal, this is the spot.

  • yummy yummy bakery

    I vouch for their pineapple buns & egg tarts: pineapple buns because they are delicious and egg tarts because I can never turn one down.
