How the World Sees AI

github. app.

work in progress

AI can be confusing when it shouldn’t be. For anyone who’s curious about learning more about the terminology and mechanisms of AI, I aim to explain it top-down: take what we have experience with (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) and break it down into smaller pieces. From model to agent to harness, this is an interactive way to explore what AI is, how it works, and what are open problems to solve.
